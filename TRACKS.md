## Sponsor Tracks

### [Title Sponsor: Meta / Facebook] Bringing People Closer Together with AI
- **Prize:** Top 3 teams receive an invitation to advance to the Round 2 Hackathon at Meta's Menlo Park campus (travel & accommodations provided) + Meta swag bag.
- **Prompt:** Build the AI-powered social product you wish existed, or one that strengthens real human connections in new ways (e.g., synthesizing group discussions into shared plans, organizing scattered family updates into a shared story, identifying common interests to spark new connections).
- **Criteria:** How meaningfully it strengthens human connection, how essential and well-integrated AI is, originality, and strength of the working demo.
- **Recommended Meta AI Tools & APIs:**
  - **Muse Spark:** Meta's text model for chat, reasoning, and agentic experiences.
  - **Muse Image:** Meta's image-generation model.
  - **Muse Voice Transcribe:** Meta's real-time audio perception model.
  - **Muse:** Meta's personal AI agent powered by Muse Spark (supports custom connectors exposing project data/actions as callable tools).
  - **Facebook Graph API:** Access public and user-consented social data.

### [SerpApi] Best Use of SerpApi
- **Prize:** ARZOPA Portable Monitors
- **Prompt:** Build something useful or creative where SerpApi plays a meaningful role. SerpApi provides 100+ real-time APIs across web search, travel (hotels, flights, maps, places), e-commerce, finance, and academic research, plus an Extract API for web page content.

### [NMC2] Build the Platform Behind the Agent
- **Prize:** 1st: AirPods Max Gen 2 | 2nd: Apple Watch Series 12 | 3rd: Echo Show 11
- **Prompt:** Building an AI agent involves repeating setup (connecting tools, configuring models, writing instructions, adding monitoring). Design and prototype a reusable platform that lets users rapidly create, configure, and monitor enterprise-grade AI agents.

### [Capital One] The Best Use of Nessie
- **Prize:** $250 Giftogram gift cards (each team member)
- **Prompt:** Reimagine the banking and personal finance experience using Capital One's Nessie API (`api.nessieisreal.com`), which provides mock financial data including accounts, merchants, bills, and P2P transactions.

### [Toyota Financial Services] "What's Your Next Move?" Challenge
- **Prize:** 1st: $500 Amazon Gift Card | 2nd: $350 Amazon Gift Card | 3rd: $150 Amazon Gift Card
- **Prompt:** Help Toyota customers answer: *"Given my situation today, what's the smartest next move for me and my Toyota?"*
  - **Personalize:** Use a simulated customer profile, vehicle, and loan/lease details to generate insights.
  - **Simplify:** Explain realistic options, payments, costs, assumptions, and trade-offs in plain language.
  - **Guide:** Guide customers to a clear next step (self-service, follow-up, or eligible offer).
  - **Value:** Demonstrate how it improves retention, grows relevant revenue, or reduces service effort.
  - **Expectations:** Working prototype, proved with synthetic data, responsible AI innovation, reimagined customer experience, clear business value.
